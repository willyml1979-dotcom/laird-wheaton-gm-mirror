Your daily drive can be electric without becoming complicated. The new 2027 Chevrolet Bolt RS brings electric driving into a practical five passenger SUV, combining 150 kW DC fast charging capability with everyday comfort and driver assistance technology. The result feels particularly well suited to the kilometres that make up life on Vancouver Island. Morning across Nanaimo. An afternoon in Parksville. Dinner in Qualicum Beach. Or a Saturday where the Island Highway keeps pulling you north through Courtenay, the Comox Valley and maybe farther toward Campbell River. Some days are predictable. Others become road trips. The Bolt RS is ready for both. Its all electric drive unit is supported by a 60 to 70 kWh battery pack and 150 kW DC fast charging capability, giving you a fast charging option when the day calls for more time on the road. Inside, front bucket seats and power seat adjustment help you settle into the drive. Automatic climate control manages cabin comfort throughout the year, while a heated steering wheel is waiting for those cool Vancouver Island mornings when touching a cold steering wheel is the last thing anyone wants to do. Adaptive Cruise Control adds convenience once you settle into a longer highway stretch, while Lane Departure Warning provides additional driver awareness along the way. Rear Parking Assist Sensors help when the open road eventually leads back to a tighter parking space in town. An auto dimming rearview mirror rounds out the supplied equipment. But perhaps the most appealing part is how normal it can all feel. Five seats. Everyday comfort. Weekend versatility. Except the drive is electric.
