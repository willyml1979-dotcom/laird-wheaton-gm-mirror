Under the hood, the responsive 2.5 litre turbocharged engine provides impressive power when merging onto the highway or climbing Island backroads, while all wheel drive helps inspire confidence when the weather changes. The refined suspension delivers a comfortable ride whether you are commuting through town or setting out for a longer road trip with family and friends. Inside, the Sorento EX offers a thoughtfully designed cabin with room for up to seven passengers, making it just as capable for school drop offs as it is for weekend escapes.
